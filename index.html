GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about the QRHub generator.

Is QRHub really free?

Yes. QR code generation and downloads are free, with no watermark, subscription or registration.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Open the page, create your QR code and download it.

Do QR codes expire?

QRHub creates static QR codes. They do not expire on a QRHub schedule, but the destination or linked content can change or become unavailable.

Which formats can I download?

PNG, JPG and SVG. SVG is useful for print and PNG is useful for web and everyday sharing.

Can I add a logo?

Yes. Upload an image to place it in the center. Always test-scan the finished code before printing.

How does the PDF QR code work?

Upload your PDF to a file host that provides a public link, then paste that link into the PDF QR tool.
